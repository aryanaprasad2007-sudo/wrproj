What we build

Residential general contracting across the Bay Area — from a single bathroom to a house that doesn't exist yet.

01 — Interior

Whole Interior Remodeling

When a house has good bones and a bad layout, this is the fix. We take the interior back to the framing and rebuild it around how you actually live — which usually means opening up the kitchen, rethinking where the walls are, and replacing systems that were installed before anyone worried about them.

  • Layout changes and load-bearing wall removal with engineered beams
  • Full electrical rewiring and panel upgrades
  • Repiping, drain lines, and new fixtures
  • Drywall, trim, doors, flooring, and paint
  • Insulation, windows, and interior finishes

Typical timeline: 8–16 weeks depending on square footage and permit review.

02 — Kitchens & Baths

Kitchens & Bathrooms

These are the rooms people notice and the rooms where bad work shows up fastest — a shower pan that wasn't sloped right or a cabinet run that isn't level will bother you every day for twenty years. We take our time on the parts nobody sees, because that's the part that lasts.

  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry, islands, and pantry builds
  • Quartz, granite, and solid surface countertops
  • Tile showers with proper waterproofing and sloped pans
  • Heated floors, steam showers, and free-standing tubs
  • Ventilation, lighting design, and appliance rough-in

Typical timeline: 3–6 weeks for a bathroom, 6–10 for a kitchen.

03 — Additions

Home Additions

Growing family, aging parent, or a home office that isn't a corner of the bedroom. An addition is the cheapest square footage you'll ever buy in this market — but only if the new part is tied into the old one correctly, structurally and visually.

  • Room additions and bump-outs
  • Second-story additions
  • Foundation work, framing, and roof tie-ins
  • Matching existing siding, roofline, and trim details
  • Extending HVAC, electrical, and plumbing to the new space

Typical timeline: 4–8 months including design and permitting.

04 — ADUs

ADUs & Garage Conversions

California law now makes it far easier to add a second unit to a residential lot, and most Bay Area cities have streamlined their approvals to match. An ADU can house family, generate rent, or add real value to the property — and we build all three kinds.

  • Detached ADUs — a standalone unit in the back yard, foundation to finish
  • Attached ADUs — built off the existing house with a separate entrance
  • Garage conversions — turning existing square footage into a legal unit
  • JADUs — junior units carved out of the existing footprint
  • Separate utility metering, kitchens, and full bathrooms

We handle the plan set, the city submittal, and the inspections. You should not have to learn your city's ADU ordinance to get one built.

Typical timeline: 6–12 months from first drawing to final inspection.

05 — Ground Up

New Construction

A lot, a set of plans, and someone who has done it enough times to know what the plans left out. We build single-family homes from grading through final — and we're just as happy working from an architect you already hired as bringing our own.

  • Site prep, grading, and foundations
  • Framing, roofing, and building envelope
  • Complete mechanical, electrical, and plumbing
  • Title 24 energy compliance
  • Full interior and exterior finishes

Typical timeline: 10–18 months depending on jurisdiction and scope.
