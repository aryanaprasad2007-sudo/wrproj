About

Simon Latchman

Owner of Bay One Construction & Remodeling, and the person who will actually be standing in your kitchen.

Twenty-five years of Bay Area homes

Simon (Mandeep) Latchman has been building and remodeling in the Bay Area for more than twenty-five years. In that time the region has changed enormously — the codes, the permit process, what materials cost, what homeowners want out of a house. What hasn't changed is the part that decides whether a remodel goes well: whether the person who sold you the job is the person who runs it.

Bay One is deliberately not a large operation. Simon quotes the work, orders the material, schedules the trades, and is on site while it's being built. If something goes sideways at 7am, you're calling the owner, not a coordinator who has to check with someone else.

That's the whole business model, and it's why most of our work comes from homeowners who used us before or heard about us from a neighbor.

How we work

What you can count on

An honest number

Our estimates are itemized and we stand behind them. If we think your budget doesn't match your scope, we'll say so before you spend money on drawings.

Licensed and insured

Fully licensed with the California State License Board, bonded, and carrying both liability and workers' compensation coverage. Ask to see any of it.

Permits pulled properly

Unpermitted work is a problem you inherit at resale. We pull permits and meet the inspector, every time.

A clean job site

Dust barriers, floor protection, and debris hauled out. You are living in this house while we work in it.

Changes in writing

Scope changes get priced and signed before the work happens, so the final invoice doesn't surprise anyone.

We finish

The last ten percent of a remodel is where a lot of contractors disappear. We do the punch list and we close out the permit.
