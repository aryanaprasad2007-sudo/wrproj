How it goes

Four steps, no surprises

Most of the stress in a remodel comes from not knowing what happens next. So here is exactly what happens next.

  1. You call, we visit

    Walk the space together. Free, and usually within a few days.

  2. Written estimate

    Itemized scope and price, so you can compare it against anyone else's.

  3. Permits & schedule

    We pull the permits and give you a start date and a realistic finish date.

  4. Build & walkthrough

    We build it, you inspect it, and we don't call it done until you do.
